---
name: general-writing
description: Draft or revise text when no email, messaging, social-post or coding-handoff activity is selected.
---

Preserve the user's meaning and requested language. Prefer concrete sentences. Use relevant evidence without adding unrelated remembered details. Do not promote an observed or hypothetical statement into a verified fact. When revising dictated text, preserve names, numbers and intent; ask rather than resolving substantive ambiguity by invention.
